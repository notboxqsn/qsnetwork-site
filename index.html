What we do

Trust infrastructure, delivered as a service.

Organizations hold records that others need to trust — supplier standing, licences, registrations, certifications. QSNetwork turns those records into credentials that prove themselves, backed by resilient decentralized data infrastructure.

Core service

Verifiable credentials & digital trust

An end-to-end pipeline from authoritative data to instantly verifiable proof — with no manual processing at any step.

  • Automated ingestion of authoritative and public datasets — credentials stay current without staff action
  • Credentials signed inside hardware Trusted Execution Environments — keys never leave the enclave
  • Passkey sign-in for credential holders — Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello. No wallet software, no app
  • Embeddable live badges and QR verification — any relying party confirms status in under a second
Foundation service

Decentralized storage & data infrastructure

The same production network that anchors our credentials is available for resilient data services in its own right.

  • Distributed storage across 800+ independent nodes — no single point of failure
  • Tamper-evident ledger anchoring for records that must survive audit
  • Decentralized database and compute layers for data-intensive workloads

Security & privacy by design

Nothing to install. Nothing sensitive to hold.

The platform is designed so any organization can evaluate and adopt it without an IT project.

Browser-only delivery

A hosted service. No hardware, no software installation, no client infrastructure required to issue or verify.

No access to internal networks

The platform operates on the data sources you designate, through public or published endpoints. It never connects inside your network.

No biometric or confidential data

Passkey biometrics stay on the user's device. The platform processes only the record data needed to issue a credential — nothing personal beyond it.

Accessible and bilingual

User-facing portals target WCAG 2.1 AA, operate in English and French, and are optimized for low-bandwidth connections.
