The innovation

From public data to verified credential — with no staff action.

DSTN answers problem statement 20.1.a of EN578-26ISC1: give the Government of Canada a way to issue secure, tamper-resistant supplier credentials that suppliers can display and buyers can verify — without manual intervention.

STEP 1 — INGEST

Public contract data, daily

DSTN ingests CanadaBuys Contract History and Open Government procurement datasets every day and derives each supplier's active status automatically.

zero staff action
STEP 2 — ISSUE

Credential signed in hardware

An issuer agent running inside a Trusted Execution Environment signs a W3C-aligned AnonCreds credential anchored on the Stratos blockchain. Signing keys never leave the enclave.

tamper-resistant
STEP 3 — VERIFY

One click. Under one second.

Suppliers sign in with a passkey — Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello — and embed a live badge on their website. Buyers verify with a click or QR scan.

< 1s cryptographic proof

Built for government testing

Nothing to install. Nothing sensitive to hold.

DSTN was designed so a federal department can evaluate it without an IT project.

Browser-only for testers

Delivered as a hosted service. No hardware, no software installation, no department infrastructure required.

No access to government networks

DSTN operates entirely on public data and public endpoints. It never connects to internal GoC systems.

No biometric or confidential data

Passkey biometrics stay on the user's device. DSTN processes only public procurement data — nothing personal, nothing confidential.

Accessible and bilingual

The Supplier Portal targets WCAG 2.1 AA, operates in English and French, and is optimized for low-bandwidth connections.

Technology foundation

Running on production infrastructure, not a prototype cloud.

DSTN is built on Stratos — a decentralized cloud network operated in production since 2021, combining blockchain, storage, compute with hardware TEE capability, and database layers. The credential protocol follows open standards, so nothing about DSTN locks the government in.

Interested in testing DSTN?

Federal departments and agencies can request to test DSTN through the Innovative Solutions Canada program. Testing is funded by ISC — up to $2.3M — and requires no procurement vehicle from your department.

Departments should contact the ISC program directly; the program matches testers with conditionally qualified innovations.
